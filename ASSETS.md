## Banners

Wide (16:9) pieces in the brand-art style: deep blue, one violet-to-sun light source, grain. Originals in
`assets-src/banners/` (not deployed); web copies in `public/banners/` (deployed, but a visitor only downloads what a
page uses).

| Name | Still | Moving | Look |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `capsule` | yes | yes | Dark blue left, glowing capsule and spheres right. Text sits well on the left. |
| `subtle` | yes | yes | Blue tubes and a sun ring drifting. Calmest of the moving ones. |
| `orange` | yes | yes | Mostly orange, blue ribbons right. Loud: white text fails contrast on it. |
| `wave` | yes | no | Cream top fading into blue. Its top blends into `--paper`. |

Web copies:
- Stills: `<name>-800.webp` and `<name>-1600.webp` (~6-23 KB), via
  `sharp(src).resize({ width }).webp({ quality: 74 })`.
- Moving: `<name>.mp4`, 1248×704, 20 s, no audio (~0.75 MB). The 10 s original plays forward then backward so the
  loop has no jump; H.264 CRF 30, `+faststart`. Use the matching still as the poster, load only when near the
  viewport, pause offscreen, and show the still under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

Third-party app names and icons are used only to show which apps Hopper works with (nominative use); the footer says
so. Before launch, re-check each brand's guidelines (Apple's are the strictest) and swap any tile whose owner asks.
