Every browser has a Back button. It’s the most used button on the web. Your Mac, the thing that holds all those browsers and everything else, doesn’t have one.

## What “Back” should mean on a Mac

Here’s a normal minute. You’re in a Notion doc. A Slack message pulls you into a thread. The thread links a PR. The PR mentions a dashboard. Now you want to get back to the doc.

<kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>Tab</kbd> gets you to Notion, the app. But which page? Which window? You’re hunting again.

That’s the gap. **Your Mac remembers apps. It should remember places.** A place is a tab, a doc, a window or a coding session. Back should take you to the place.

## Back and Forward, everywhere

With [Hopper](/features/navigation), press <kbd>⌃</kbd><kbd>⌥</kbd><kbd>←</kbd> from any app and you’re back where you were: that doc, in that window. Press it again to keep going back. <kbd>⌃</kbd><kbd>⌥</kbd><kbd>→</kbd> undoes a Back.

It works across apps the way a browser works across pages. No switcher to scroll through.

## Two flips for the back-and-forth

Most switching is between two things. So there are two gestures for it, both double taps:

- **Toggle**: double-tap <kbd>⌘</kbd> to flip between your two most recent **apps**.
- **Toggle Tab**: double-tap <kbd>⌥</kbd> to flip between your two most recent **tabs, windows or sessions**, even inside one app. Two Chrome tabs, two Claude Code sessions, a doc and its spec.

Writing code against a spec? Double-tap <kbd>⌥</kbd>, read, double-tap again.

## Three more ways back

- **History** (press and release <kbd>⌃</kbd><kbd>⌘</kbd>): every place you visited, newest first. Kept for 30 days by default, or as long as you choose.
- **Recently Closed**: close a tab by accident and it waits here, ready to reopen in its own browser.
- **Now Playing**: jump straight to whatever is making sound, like the tab with the video, or Music. If several things are playing, pick from a list.

All of these are on the [Hotkeys & Gestures cheat sheet](/features/hotkeys).
